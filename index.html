Method: a die counts when all four corners lie inside the usable area, which is the wafer outline (circle and any flats) moved inward by the edge exclusion. Dies sit on one regular grid aligned with the major flat, with straight X and Y streets running across the whole wafer, as a dicing saw cuts them. The staggered option models plasma dicing: streets run straight in one direction only and each row (or column) is placed on its own, with the shared offset chosen exactly. The optimal placement is exact rather than sampled: an optimal grid can always be slid down and then left until one die, or a pair of dies, meets the edge of the usable area. That area is convex, flats included, so checking the lowest-left position of every die pair finds an optimum, to within 10⁻⁸ mm. The area-only estimate is usable area divided by pitch area, not an exact packing limit.
