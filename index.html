Method: counts axis-aligned dies on a regular rectangular grid only when all four die corners fall within the usable circular radius. Scribe streets add spacing between dies. The area-only estimate is usable wafer area divided by pitch area, not an exact packing limit. This is a circular wafer model: flats, notches, alignment marks, test patterns, and manufacturing yield are not modeled.
